I am a Senior Solutions Architect at adidas, specialising in AI. I design scalable machine learning solutions that bridge business requirements with technical implementation, drawing on years of work in data analytics and simulation.

My focus is end-to-end ML pipelines, cloud infrastructure tuned for AI workloads, and making sure what we ship delivers measurable business value. I also mentor teams in data science and ML practice.

## What I work with

- **Cloud AI and MLOps.** ML pipeline design, model deployment and monitoring, lifecycle management, and solution architecture on AWS, Databricks and Google Cloud.
- **Programming.** Python for ML and backend services, SQL for analytics, Docker and Kubernetes for ML workloads.
- **Data.** Business intelligence, simulation and scenario analysis, supervised and unsupervised learning, ETL and ELT pipeline design.

## Beyond work

Writing about technology, mentoring people starting out in data science, and reading about where AI and cloud computing are heading.
